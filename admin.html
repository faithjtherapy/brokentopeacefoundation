The original plan’s first steps: corporate records (A1–A8), charitable registration (A9), federal application preparation (B1–B6), website basics (G1–G3), and licensing orientation (D1–D2).

Workstream A

Corporate records

Confirm the organization’s identity and assemble its governing records.

0 of 9 done
A1Confirm the legal entity name

Check the exact registered name against Illinois Secretary of State records.

A2Obtain a current good-standing certificate

Verify current good-standing status with the Illinois Secretary of State and obtain a fresh certificate.

A3Confirm the registered agent

Check that the agent and address on file are current; arrange an update if needed.

A4Locate the Articles of Incorporation

Collect the original articles and any amendments for the organization’s records and exemption application.

A5Review bylaws and governance policies

Locate or draft the bylaws and conflict-of-interest policy. Confirm which provisions belong in the articles, bylaws, or separate policies with an adviser.

A6Locate the EIN confirmation

Find the EIN notice or ask the IRS about obtaining confirmation. Record the document’s location in notes.

A7Confirm the board and officer roster

Collect names and roles. Verify applicable board-composition and independence requirements instead of relying on the original checklist’s shorthand.

A8Resolve annual corporate filings

Check the organization’s filing history and confirm any missing reports, current forms, and fees.

A9Complete Illinois charitable registration

Confirm the applicable registration requirements, current forms, attachments, and fees with the Attorney General before beginning fundraising.

Workstream B

Federal recognition

Prepare the application for federal tax-exempt recognition.

0 of 10 done
B1Choose the appropriate IRS application

Use the current IRS guidance to compare Form 1023 and Form 1023-EZ. Confirm eligibility, user fees, and current processing expectations.

B2Complete the current 1023-EZ eligibility worksheet

Use the full current worksheet. Financial thresholds alone do not determine eligibility; confirm any program or entity exclusions.

B3Draft the narrative of activities

Describe past, current, and planned activities. Use the founder’s original program discussion as source material, then obtain her approval.

B4Prepare a three-year budget

Build realistic revenue and expense projections and confirm the financial information required for the chosen application.

B5Review charitable-purpose and dissolution provisions

Have an adviser check the organizing documents, dissolution provisions, and conflict-of-interest policy against current application requirements.

B6Submit the approved IRS application

Prepare the appropriate application and submit through Pay.gov after the supporting information and authorization are ready.

B7Confirm prior exemption history and effective-date rules

Determine whether an exemption ever existed or was revoked. Confirm whether initial recognition, an effective-date request, or reinstatement rules apply.

B8Save the IRS determination letter

After approval, retain the determination letter with the legal records and record its location.

B9Confirm and apply for Illinois sales-tax exemption

Check the current Illinois Department of Revenue process and supporting documents, including federal recognition where applicable.

B10Confirm Illinois income-tax obligations

Verify the organization’s state tax treatment and any filing requirements with an accountant.

Workstream C

Banking

Put the organization’s financial accounts and controls in place.

0 of 5 done
C1Choose a bank and gather opening documents

Confirm account terms and the bank’s required documents, such as the EIN notice, articles, bylaws, and authorizing board resolution. Bank of America was one option in the original plan.

C2Approve account signers and controls

Choose authorized signers with the board and document payment approvals and separation of responsibilities.

C3Arrange needed cards and checks

After the account is open, order only the payment tools the organization needs and document who may use them.

C4Connect banking and bookkeeping

Set up authorized online banking and the planned QuickBooks connection.

C5Plan restricted-fund tracking

Agree with the accountant how restricted funds will be tracked, including whether separate bank accounts are appropriate.

Workstream D

Group home licensing

Prepare the planned home for girls ages 12 to 16.

0 of 14 done
D1Request a licensing orientation

Contact DCFS about the new-applicant process and confirm the correct license category for the proposed home.

D2Prepare the program letter of intent

Describe the proposed population, capacity, and location. Confirm the required submission format with the licensing team.

D3Complete required background checks

Ask DCFS which checks apply to each staff, board, and applicant role, and track completion through the approved process.

D4Confirm and complete required training

Identify current pre-service training requirements by role and retain completion records.

D5Identify a suitable property

Check zoning, occupancy, accessibility, and life-safety requirements with the relevant authorities before committing to a site.

D6Arrange fire and occupancy approvals

Confirm the required fire inspection and occupancy documentation for the chosen property and license category.

D7Arrange health and safety inspections

Confirm applicable inspection requirements for food preparation, bedrooms, bathrooms, and other facilities.

D8Prepare required program policies

Cover admissions, discharge, education, health, medication, sexuality and pregnancy support, grievances, incident reporting, discipline, and records. Confirm the full required set under the applicable licensing rules.

D9Develop the staffing plan

Plan 24/7 coverage and clinical support; verify required staffing ratios and qualifications with DCFS.

D10Verify leadership qualifications

Confirm current requirements for the executive and program leadership roles for the chosen license category.

D11Arrange appropriate insurance

Review general and professional liability, directors and officers, transportation, and abuse/molestation coverage with a qualified broker.

D12Submit the licensing application

Assemble the complete application and supporting records using the current DCFS process.

D13Complete the licensing inspection

Arrange the on-site inspection and resolve documented follow-up items.

D14Complete licensing and placement-contract steps

Receive the applicable license, then separately confirm contracting and placement approvals. A license alone does not establish a placement contract.

Workstream E

Transitional living

Plan the next stage for ages 17 to 19; timing and eligibility need confirmation.

0 of 7 done
E1Confirm the transitional living application path

Year two is the program’s planning target. Ask DCFS about prerequisites and timing; do not assume a mandatory twelve-month wait.

E2Confirm the legal structure for the second program

Determine whether a separate entity or amendments to current documents are needed.

E3Develop a transitional living site plan

Plan the semi-independent setting and verify the requirements for the applicable transitional living license.

E4Write an individualized transition policy

Plan continuity for the intended ages 17 to 19 while confirming DCFS eligibility and each resident’s transition requirements.

E5Develop the independent-living curriculum

Include daily living skills, budgeting, employment preparation, education, and continued support.

E6Complete the second program’s readiness steps

Confirm required inspections, background checks, staffing, insurance, and approvals for transitional living.

E7Confirm the licensing path for ages 18 to 19

Part 409 (Youth Transitional Living) caps eligibility at less than 18 years of age, so ages 18 and 19 fall outside it. Confirm with DCFS whether Part 409 fits the intended 17-to-19 band or whether a separate path is required for residents 18 and older. Federal Runaway and Homeless Youth Act transitional living grants (ages 16 to 21) and state extended-foster-care arrangements are among the options to evaluate.

F7Confirm fundraising registration readiness

Coordinate with A9 and verify applicable registration requirements before soliciting contributions.
